🍜 Chinese Chow Mein

Ingredients:

  • Chow mein/Hakka noodles – 200 g
  • Cabbage – 1 cup, finely chopped
  • Carrot – ½ cup, finely chopped
  • Capsicum – ½ cup, finely chopped
  • Spring onion – ½ cup, chopped
  • Garlic – 1 tbsp, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1½ tbsp
  • Vinegar – 1 tsp
  • Black pepper – ½ tsp
  • Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the noodles and toss them with a little oil to prevent sticking.
  3. Heat oil in a wok/kadhai on high flame.
  4. Add garlic and stir-fry for 20–30 seconds.
  5. Add cabbage, carrot and capsicum. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
  6. Add the boiled noodles.
  7. Add soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper and salt.
  8. Toss everything on high flame for 2–3 minutes.
  9. Add spring onion and serve hot.

Cooking the noodles separately and stir-frying them quickly on high flame helps maintain their texture.
