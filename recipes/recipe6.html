🍗 Chicken Manchurian

Ingredients:

  • Boneless chicken – 300 g, cut into small pieces
  • Cornflour – 3 tbsp
  • Maida – 2 tbsp
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 1 tsp
  • Black pepper – ½ tsp
  • Garlic – 1 tbsp, chopped
  • Ginger – 1 tsp, chopped
  • Spring onion – ½ cup, chopped
  • Capsicum – ½ cup, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Chilli sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Tomato ketchup – 1 tbsp
  • Vinegar – 1 tsp
  • Cornflour – 1 tbsp
  • Water – ½ cup
  • Oil – for frying
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Mix chicken with cornflour, maida, ginger-garlic paste, black pepper and salt.
  2. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  3. Deep-fry the chicken pieces until golden and crispy.
  4. Remove the fried chicken and keep it aside.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp oil in another wok/kadhai.
  6. Add chopped garlic and ginger and stir-fry for a few seconds.
  7. Add spring onion and capsicum and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  8. Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup and vinegar.
  9. Add ½ cup water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  10. Mix 1 tbsp cornflour with a little water and add it to the sauce.
  11. Cook until the sauce becomes slightly thick.
  12. Add the fried chicken and toss well.
  13. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.

For crispy Chicken Manchurian, fry the chicken until golden before adding it to the thick and flavourful sauce.
