🥢 Chinese Spring Rolls

Ingredients:

  • Spring roll wrappers – 8–10
  • Cabbage – 1 cup, finely shredded
  • Carrot – ½ cup, finely chopped
  • Capsicum – ½ cup, finely chopped
  • Spring onion – ¼ cup, chopped
  • Garlic – 1 tsp, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Black pepper – ½ tsp
  • Cornflour – 1 tbsp
  • Water – 2 tbsp
  • Oil – for frying
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok/kadhai.
  2. Add garlic and stir-fry for a few seconds.
  3. Add cabbage, carrot and capsicum.
  4. Add soy sauce, black pepper and salt.
  5. Cook the vegetables on high flame for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Allow the filling to cool completely.
  7. Place a spring roll wrapper on a flat surface and add the vegetable filling.
  8. Fold the sides and roll the wrapper tightly.
  9. Mix cornflour with water to make a paste and use it to seal the edges.
  10. Deep-fry the spring rolls until golden and crispy.
  11. Serve hot with chilli sauce.

The filling should not contain excess water because a dry filling helps the spring rolls remain crispy.
