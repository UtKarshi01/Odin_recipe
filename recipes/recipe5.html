🌶️ Kung Pao Chicken

Ingredients:

  • Boneless chicken – 300 g, cut into small pieces
  • Peanuts – ½ cup, roasted
  • Dried red chillies – 5–7
  • Capsicum – ½ cup, chopped
  • Onion – ½ cup, chopped
  • Garlic – 1 tbsp, chopped
  • Ginger – 1 tsp, finely chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Vinegar – 1 tbsp
  • Cornflour – 1 tbsp
  • Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Coat the chicken pieces lightly with cornflour.
  2. Heat oil in a wok/kadhai on medium-high flame.
  3. Add the chicken pieces and stir-fry until they are cooked and lightly browned.
  4. Remove the chicken from the wok and keep it aside.
  5. Add dried red chillies, garlic and ginger to the same wok.
  6. Add onion and capsicum and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes.
  7. Add the cooked chicken back into the wok.
  8. Add soy sauce, vinegar and salt.
  9. Add roasted peanuts and toss everything together.
  10. Cook for another 1–2 minutes on high flame.
  11. Serve hot with fried rice or noodles.

Stir-frying the ingredients on high flame helps retain the texture of the vegetables and gives the dish a smoky flavour.
