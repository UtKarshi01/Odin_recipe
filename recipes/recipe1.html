Chinese Vegetable Fried Rice

\

Ingredients:

  • Cooked rice – 2 cups
  • Carrot – ½ cup, finely chopped
  • Capsicum – ½ cup
  • Cabbage – ½ cup
  • Spring onion – ½ cup
  • Garlic – 1 tbsp, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Vinegar – 1 tsp
  • Black/white pepper – ½ tsp
  • Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Heat oil in a wok/kadhai on high flame.
  2. Add garlic and stir-fry for 20–30 seconds.
  3. Add carrot, cabbage and capsicum. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Add cooked, preferably cold rice.
  5. Add soy sauce, vinegar, pepper and salt.
  6. Toss everything on high flame for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Add spring onion and serve hot.

Cold/leftover rice works particularly well because the grains stay separate during stir-frying.
