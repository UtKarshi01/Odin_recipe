🍲 Hot & Sour Soup

Ingredients:

  • Vegetable or chicken stock – 3 cups
  • Mushrooms – ½ cup, sliced
  • Carrot – ¼ cup, finely chopped
  • Cabbage – ¼ cup, finely chopped
  • Tofu – ½ cup, cubed
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Vinegar – 1–1½ tbsp
  • Black/white pepper – ½ tsp
  • Cornflour – 1½ tbsp
  • Water – 3 tbsp
  • Spring onion – for garnish
  • Salt – as needed

Method:

  1. Heat the vegetable or chicken stock in a large pan and bring it to a boil.
  2. Add mushrooms, carrot, cabbage and tofu.
  3. Cook the vegetables for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper and salt.
  5. Mix cornflour with water in a separate bowl to make a smooth slurry.
  6. Slowly add the cornflour slurry to the soup while stirring continuously.
  7. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the soup becomes slightly thick.
  8. Add chopped spring onion.
  9. Serve hot.

The balance between vinegar and pepper gives the soup its characteristic hot and sour flavour.
